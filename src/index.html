WHAT THIS NUMBER IS — AND ISN'T

Priority describes workflow delay, not a person.

The index uses stage age, the applicable limit, an overdue recorded commitment, waiting responsibility, and contact age. It never predicts quality, interest, performance, or hire probability.

AUDIT VIEW / VERSION 1

How Driftline decides

01

Validate first

Required fields, ISO dates, recognized stages, waiting responsibility, future movement dates, and duplicate IDs are checked before ranking. Invalid rows stay visible.

02

Apply a stage-aware limit

The base limit is multiplied by a visible stage factor and rounded up. Offer is 0.5×; assessment is 1.5×; applied, recruiter screen, and interview are 1×.

03

Rank the follow-up load

Index = floor(100 × days ÷ limit) + 30 for an overdue promise + 15/8/0 for company/external/candidate responsibility + contact age capped at 20.

04

Keep the decision human

Fixed templates explain the facts and suggest an action. Driftline never advances, rejects, messages, or edits a candidate record.

Same input + same settings = same queue.
